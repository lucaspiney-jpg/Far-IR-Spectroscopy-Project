Abstract - 

AFGL 618 is a carbon-rich protoplanetary nebula (PPN) that has recently
left the asymptotic giant branch (AGB) and is quickly heading towards the
planetary nebula (PN) phase. The data used in this experiment come from
a FITS file of an observation of AFGL 618 from the Herschel SPIRE-FTS
instrument containing ten 12CO lines from J = 4 → 3 to 13 → 12. I subtracted
the continuum, corrected the CO J = 10 → 9 line for an HCN overlap, fitted
sinc functions to each of the lines, found the Doppler shift of the centres and
plotted a rotation diagram. I found that AFGL 618 has a systemic velocity
vLSR = −21.9 ± 1.8 km s−1 (vhelio = −14.7 ± 1.8 km s−1), agreeing with the
literature value to 0.2σ. A single-temperature fit failed to represent the data,
shown by the extremely high rediced χ2 of 175; however a two-temperature fit with
a cool and hot component of 34 ± 4 K and 240 ± 9 K respectively gave a
far lower reduced χ2 = 6.7. The widths of the lines were found to be 1.5% broader
than the instrumental resolution, and the continuum index varied between
the low- and high-frequency bands. My results suggest that AFGL 618 has a
high-temperature fast outflow and a cooler outer envelope, giving the PPN a
range of temperatures that get hotter towards the centre.
